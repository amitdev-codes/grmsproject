**DEVELOPMENT OF A MOBILE BASED AND WEB-BASED GRIEVANCE REDRESS MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (GRMS) AND UPGRADING OF THE ROADS DIRECTORATE WEBSITE** 

**NOVEMBER 2025** 

# **TERMS OF REFERENCE** 

## **1. BACKGROUND** 

The government of Lesotho has received funding from the International Development Association (IDA) through the World Bank for the Lesotho Integrated Transport Trade and Logistics (LITTL) project and intends to utilise part of the funds in development of a mobile based and web-based Grievance Redress Management System (GRMS) and upgrading of the Roads Directorate website.  The Roads Directorate (RD) was established by legislation (Roads Directorate Act 2010) as a semi-autonomous institution under the Ministry of Public Works and Transport. It was established primarily for better service delivery and more effective and efficient management of the Lesotho road network. The Directorate is responsible for construction, upgrading, rehabilitation and maintenance of primary, secondary, tertiary and other roads as well as bridges on the Lesotho Road Network. 

The Roads Directorate aims to improve transparency, accountability and stakeholder engagement in service delivery.  As part of this objective, RD identified a need for an inclusive, robust, user friendly and secure mobile based (Android system, IOS optional) and web-based Grievance Redress Management System (GRMS), to receive, track and resolve grievances from the public and stakeholders in a timely and effective manner.  For system purposes, the term grievance, will encompass a broad range of inputs including complaints, feedback, suggestions, recommendations, and compliments. 

A holistic policy compliant system shall be developed to ensure amongst others, full compliance with Lesotho’s disability and gender policies and to support multi-channel grievance submission and management.  The system shall be designed with universal accessibility features to ensure inclusive access for all users, including people with disabilities, women, and rural communities.  The holistic citizen engagement framework will facilitate community participation by enabling individuals to; i) share success stories, positive experiences and best practices, ii) provide constructive input to improve service delivery and iii) contribute innovative ideas that may not be captured through traditional and formal channels 

The current grievance management process operates manually and does not provide real time monitoring or tracking capabilities. To increase efficiency, accessibility and functionality, the existing manual records will be digitized and seamlessly integrated into the new system.  The system will continue to support multi-channel submissions, therefore complementing the traditional mechanisms for lodging grievances through the local authorities (chiefs, community councillors). Additional submission options include a) Community Liaison Officers (CLO), b) Roads Directorate offices, d) Grievance boxes, e) Grievance Redress Committees (GRC) or Multi stakeholder Community Forums and f) Roads Directorate website.  The system shall incorporate clear escalation procedures, allowing for grievances to be advanced internally if unresolved at the local level, and externally escalated when all internal processes have been exhausted and the complainant remains unsatisfied with the proposed resolution and outcome. 

While significant barriers continue to limit social inclusion and universal access to technologies, the National Disability Mainstreaming Plan (2021–2025), the Gender and Development Policy (2018–2030), and the National Strategic Development Plan II (NSDP II) demonstrate a strong commitment to inclusive development.  However, access to grievance 

redress mechanisms remains restricted particularly to People with Disabilities (PWDs), women, and rural communities.  Given that over 69% of the population resides in rural areas and a digital divide exists between urban and rural communities, there is a critical need for digitally inclusive GRMS that promotes and ensures equitable access to services.  The established system promotes inclusivity by ensuring active representation of women and youth within the GRC structure. 

Given the scale and impact of road projects on local communities and road users, it is essential to establish a responsive and transparent GRMS that will be integrated into the upgraded website. 

## **2. PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT** 

## **2.1  GRMS** 

To design, develop, test and deploy a secure and scalable web-based and mobile based GRMS that allows stakeholders to submit grievances, track the status of their grievances and receive timely responses on grievances related to road projects and its operations. The main objective of the assignment is the development and implementation of an intelligent, transparent, and efficient system that automates the handling of public grievances related to road infrastructure, ensuring timely redressal and improved service delivery. 

## **2.2  WEBSITE** 

The objective of this project is to have an inclusive excellent performance, modern look and feel, security and scalability of the website using platform independent software. The project is also intended to optimize RD online presence and performance while ensuring the highest standard of security. 

**The project aims to create a website that** : 

- Disseminates timely and accurate information about the RD, its initiatives and activities to all stakeholders. 

- Facilitates coordination and information sharing amongst all stakeholders. 

- Is comprehensive, interactive, inclusive, easy to use and navigate, aesthetically appealing and encourages repeat visits. 

- Supports RD communication and integrates existing channels of communication. 

It is against the above background that the Roads Directorate is seeking to engage the services of a **consultant to develop a web-based mobile and based Grievance Redress Management System and upgrading of the Roads Directorate website.** The consultant should have a proven track record of previous engagement in at least 3 GRMS projects of similar or complex nature than the present assignment, must have not less than five years’ experience in developing and maintaining organizational websites and must have developed at least four active websites.

- The system shall require members of the public to register to lodge a grievance 

- The system shall optimize for low-bandwidth environments 

- The system shall include offline functionality with data sync 

- The system shall require contact details of the complainant except in the case of anonymous grievance lodging 

- The system shall accept grievances lodged through different channels (such as Grievance Form, link, RD social media platforms, SMS, mobile app and USSD code) to accommodate both smartphone and non-smartphone users 

- The system shall have a Grievance Form with which to lodge a grievance 

- The system shall allow Helpdesk Officer to register lodged grievances 

- The system shall allow reporting of grievances that have been resolved informally 

- • The system shall require indication that a grievance is new or whether it is a grievance that was previously lodged or finalized 

- The system shall notify responsible manager of grievances previously lodged or finalized within a day 

- The system shall allow the allocated officers to update progress/status or grievance resolution on the system 

- The system shall register a grievance by giving it a unique number 

- The grievance shall require “Grievance category” to be selected to lodge grievance 

- The system shall have a Sesotho as a language option and Grievance Form in both Sesotho and English 

- The system shall require CAPTCHA on the grievance submission form 

- The system shall have search, filter and document/photo upload features 

- The system shall create individual Roads Directorate projects

## **b) Communication with complainant** 

- The system shall send automated message of acknowledgement of receipt of grievance to the complainant within 2 working days as stipulated in the Roads Directorate Grievance Guidelines. 

- The system shall send an automatic message with grievance number, status, an estimated timeframe for resolution as per Roads Directorate Grievance Guidelines and link to the grievance. 

- The system shall send the complainant an SMS/Email/WhatsApp Text or video to communicate a delay with grievance number, status, reason for delay, a revised estimated timeframe for resolution and a link to the grievance 

- The system shall send the complainant message to communicate that a resolution has been reached with grievance number, status, a request for complainant to sign the Sign off Form and a link to the grievance with proposed resolution. 

- The system shall allow complainant access into the system to **only view** the grievance and its status, leave a message and space/ability to e-sign after a resolution has been reached. 

- The system shall have features that allow different forms and templates to feed the database in the most efficient way, as various templates and forms are used at different stages of the complaint handling process. 

## **c) Allocation of a grievance** 

- The system shall automatically allocate grievance to the right Division by a notification using Grievance Type as categorized by Roads Directorate 

- The system shall allow manual override to responsible manager to allocate grievances to a division 

- The system shall allow Section Managers to reject an allocation and send it to the responsible manager for reallocation. System shall require a reason for rejection 

- The system shall allow Division director to allocate to a Section Manager within their Division 

- The system shall allow Section Manager to allocate to an Officer within their section to investigate and resolve the grievance. 

- The system shall allow escalation of grievances as per the Roads Directorate Grievance Guidelines 

- The system shall allow responsible Manager to close a grievance.  System should require a reason for closing. 

- The system must allow case assignment and reassignment to officers seamlessly. 

## **d) Investigation and reporting** 

- The system shall allow investigating officer to upload files. ,doc, .pdf, image 

- The system shall allow investigating officer to make a request for approval 

- The System shall require relevant manager to approve or reject proposed resolution 

- The System shall require a complainant response of proposed resolution. 

- The System shall allow creation of reports 

- The System shall allow Investigator to put grievance in “Delay” status by filling in Delay Form.  System shall send a message to complainant 

- The System shall have status that indicates a grievance lifecycle and defines the lifecycle of grievance from lodging to resolution. 

## **e) Complaint Resolution** 

- The system shall allow user to report a grievance that was resolved informally 

- • The system shall allow updating of information at every key stage of the complaint handling 

- The system shall require a resolution report in order for a grievance to be finalized 

- The system shall require an indication that complainant is satisfied by: E- signature or uploading of Sign-off Form 

- The system shall invalidate link to the grievance upon sign-off 

- The system must produce reports for management and compliance monitoring
